Soil, Sky & Hand

Sourced from sacred ground, poured with intention.

At Tea & Terroir, we believe that a cup of tea is an archive of a specific hill, a precise season, and the generational wisdom of the hands that shaped it. We bypass industrial supply chains to source directly from family estates.

Every leaf in our collection is harvested by growers who view their relationship with the earth as a long-term partnership. By paying well above fair-trade minimums, we help protect both ancestral tea forests and the communities that guard them.

The Journey of the Single Leaf

Our sourcing philosophy centers on minimal intervention and maximum respect for local ecosystems. We document each step from soil to clay cup.

1

Microclimate Sourcing

We partner exclusively with gardens at altitudes above one thousand meters, where dramatic night-and-day temperature shifts force the tea plant to concentrate its natural sugars.

2

Hand Harvest & Sifting

Machines bruise leaves and compromise clarity. Our producers hand-harvest only the top bud and first two leaves, sorting them immediately in outdoor mountain air.

3

Artisanal Wok Firing

Heat is applied carefully via wood-fired pans to stop oxidation at the perfect moment. This retains the crisp, complex minerals unique to our partner gardens.
