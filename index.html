The Terroir Philosophy

Where mountain weather meets human craft.

Just like wine, fine tea speaks of its place. The trace minerals in ancient clay soils, the morning mist that clings to steep mountain peaks, and the slow growth of cold-climate bushes create complex chemical profiles that cannot be duplicated.

At Tea & Terroir, we bypass high-yield commercial auction houses. We work hand-in-hand with individual grower-processors who let tea leaves oxidize gently in bamboo baskets, roast over pine embers, or sun-dry on mountain slopes. This results in incredibly clean infusions that can be steeped several times, revealing new flavors with each pour.
