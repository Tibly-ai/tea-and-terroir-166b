Gather at the tasting table

Our guided tea flights are quiet, immersive studies of origin. Over the course of ninety minutes, we brew single-lot harvests side-by-side using traditional gongfu vessels, tracing how mountainside microclimates, mineral soils, and careful hand-processing shape every cup.

Private Studio Flight

A dedicated tasting session for one to four guests. Your pourer will curate a progression of five exceptional, aged, or seasonal teas tailored to your palate.

Monthly Terroir Workshop

A collaborative, group-format seminar analyzing the chemical and sensory differences of tea grown across different elevations and soils.
